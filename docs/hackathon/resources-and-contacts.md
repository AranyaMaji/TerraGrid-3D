## 🛰️ Climate Data APIs & Satellite Hubs

### 1. European Space Agency & EU Copernicus
* **Copernicus Open Access Hub / Data Space:** [dataspace.copernicus.eu](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu)
  * Free access to Sentinel-1 (radar), Sentinel-2 (high-res multispectral), Sentinel-3 (thermal/ocean), Sentinel-5P (atmospheric $CH_4$, $CO$, $NO_2$).
* **Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S):** [climate.copernicus.eu](https://climate.copernicus.eu)
  * ERA5 hourly atmospheric and surface climate reanalysis.
* **Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS):** [atmosphere.copernicus.eu](https://atmosphere.copernicus.eu)
  * Solar radiation data, greenhouse gas fluxes, aerosol dispersion.
* **Copernicus Marine Environment Monitoring Service (CMEMS):** [marine.copernicus.eu](https://marine.copernicus.eu)
  * Sea surface temperature, sea level anomaly, tidal models for Pacific atolls.

### 2. Australian & Pacific Regional Data
* **OpenNEM (Australian National Electricity Market):** [opennem.org.au](https://opennem.org.au)
  * Real-time generation by fuel source (coal, gas, wind, solar, battery), spot price, and emissions intensity ($gCO_2e/kWh$).
* **Pacific Data Hub (Secretariat of the Pacific Community):** [pacificdata.org](https://pacificdata.org)
  * Ocean topography, bathymetry, disaster resilience, population vulnerability indices across 14+ Pacific nations.
* **Bureau of Meteorology (BOM) Australia Weather Data:** [bom.gov.au/climate/data](http://www.bom.gov.au/climate/data/)
  * Gridded rainfall, temperature extremes, bushfire weather indexes.
* **National Pollutant Inventory (NPI):** [npi.gov.au](https://www.npi.gov.au)
  * Point-source greenhouse gas and industrial emission reporting.

### 3. Global Climate Science Portals
* **IPCC AR6 Interactive Atlas:** [interactive-atlas.ipcc.ch](https://interactive-atlas.ipcc.ch)
  * Regional climate projections under SSP scenarios (temperature anomalies, extreme precipitation, drought).
* **Our World in Data (OWID) Energy & Climate:** [ourworldindata.org/co2-and-greenhouse-gas-emissions](https://ourworldindata.org/co2-and-greenhouse-gas-emissions)
  * Standardized CSV/JSON dumps of country-level decarbonization metrics.

## 📜 Policy & Strategic Context

* **Australia & Pacific Joint Bid for COP31:**
  * Australia submitted a joint bid with Pacific Island nations to host COP31 in late 2026.
  * Emphasizes the "Pacific COP" narrative: elevating frontline island nations whose existential survival depends on limiting warming to $1.5^\circ C$.
  * Pre-COP Leaders' meetings in Fiji and Tuvalu set regional priorities ahead of global negotiations.
* **EU Green Deal & Global Gateway:**
  * The EU's strategic framework to achieve climate neutrality by 2050 and support international clean energy partnerships.
  * EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) incentivizing green industrial standards across export partners.
