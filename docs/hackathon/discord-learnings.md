### Key Takeaways & Constraints

1. **Deadlines & Daylight Savings:**
   - **Final Submission Deadline:** Sunday, 4 Oct 2026, 21:00 AEDT (Sydney time).
   - **Daylight Savings Impact:** Southeast Australia switched to Daylight Saving Time (+1 hr) at 02:00 AM on Sunday, Oct 4. The remaining hacking window was 23 hours from the Saturday 9 PM announcement.
   - **Submissions Open Early:** Junction opened submissions at 15:00 AEDT Saturday (earlier than originally scheduled at 21:00). Submissions can and should be saved early as drafts and iteratively edited up until the 21:00 AEDT Sunday deadline.

2. **Team & Platform Rules:**
   - **Team Size:** Strict requirement of 3–5 people per team.
   - **Junction Registration:** Every team member must be registered on Junction and joined to the team via an invite code (separate from the pre-registration survey).
   - **Cross-Border Teams:** European Union participants are required to participate as part of an AU/NZ/Pacific team.

3. **Pitch & Deliverables Support:**
   - **Deliverables Focus:** Working build + 2-minute pitch video on Junction.
   - **Sunday Morning Pitch Open Mic:** Mentors are hosting an open mic review session on Sunday morning where teams can rehearse and receive direct feedback on their 2-minute pitch before final recording. First-come, first-served.

4. **Mentorship & Questions Protocol:**
   - Mentor requests must be posted publicly in `<#1541020739589767238>` (`#ask-a-mentor`), with mentor bios/schedules listed in `<#1541020695474085938>` (`#whois`). Direct messaging mentors is disallowed.
   - General hackathon questions belong in `<#1541023353492606977>` (`#event-questions`).
